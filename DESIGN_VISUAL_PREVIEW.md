### 2. Glow Orbs Animation
```
Continuous Loop:
Starting Position: (0, 0)
        ↓

        ↓
Return Position: (0, 0)

Duration: 15s or 20s (offset animations)
Easing: ease-in-out
Effect: Ambient floating glow
```
