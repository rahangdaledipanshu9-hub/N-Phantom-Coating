End-to-End Support

From formulation consultancy to on-site application and certified test protocols — we support every stage of your stealth programme.

What We Offer

Core Services

🧪

Formulation Consultancy

Custom material composition design optimised for your specific platform, threat environment, and substrate material. Includes full parameter modelling and prototype batches.

🖌️

Application Support

On-site technical assistance for surface preparation, coating application, and quality verification. Trained applicators available for critical platform programmes.

📡

EM Characterisation Testing

Full frequency-sweep S-parameter measurement (2–18 GHz) using VNA arch method. RCS evaluation and material characterisation reports provided.

🌡️

Thermal Signature Analysis

FTIR spectroscopy and infrared camera assessment to quantify thermal emissivity reduction and validate stealth performance against IR detection systems.

🔬

Mechanical & Durability Testing

Adhesion pull-off, cross-cut, impact resistance, salt spray, UV exposure, and thermal cycling tests performed to MIL-SPEC and ASTM standards.

📋

Compliance Documentation

Full qualification package including material data sheets, test reports, application procedures, and certification documentation for defence programme integration.

🎓

Training & Knowledge Transfer

Structured training programmes for coating application teams, QA inspectors, and programme managers covering all aspects of the N-Phantom system.

🏭

Scale-up & Manufacturing Support

Assistance transitioning from lab-scale formulation to production batch manufacturing, including process optimisation and quality control system implementation.
