Application Process

Clean substrate thoroughly to remove grease and contaminants. Roughen surface using grit blasting (Sa 2.5 standard). Eliminate all moisture and ensure surface is dry before coating.
Apply primer layer at 80–120 µm wet film thickness using spray or brush technique. Allow 24 hours stabilisation at 25 °C before proceeding to functional layer.
Apply functional coating at 150–250 µm wet film thickness. Ensure even coverage with no pinholes. Cure at ambient temperature (25 °C) for 48 hours minimum.
Perform adhesion cross-cut test, measure dry film thickness at multiple points, and conduct visual inspection for surface defects before system clearance.
